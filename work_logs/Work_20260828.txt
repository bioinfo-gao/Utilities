CRISPR treatment, so it is not a CRISPR-naive baseline. The usual approach of
subtracting the control from the edited sample could not be used, and the
following had to be done instead:

 - individual re-examination of every structural-variant candidate, because
   "present only in the edited sample" no longer implies "caused by the editing";
 - rework of every result and of the report text into two-sample form, with the
   sensitivity of each negative result stated explicitly.
