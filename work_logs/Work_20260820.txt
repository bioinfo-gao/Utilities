Weekly Work Report
Period: Aug 20, 2026 - Aug 27, 2026
Prepared by: Zhen Gao
Total Billable Hours: 16 Hours

1. WGS Data Processing - Yeji Park (2.5 Hours)
Processed the whole-genome samples: alignment to GRCh38, duplicate
marking, and coverage QC. Usable depth measured at 6.33x and 6.14x after
duplicate removal and read-pair overlap correction.
The submission form left the analysis purpose, the experimental design and the
guide sequences blank. The customer's reply arrived four days later, so the
first analysis had to proceed without that information.

2. First Integrative WGS Analysis - Yeji Park (4 Hours)
With no target site or construct information available, the analysis was built
on sample characteristics only: confirmation that both samples come from the
same individual, the clonal and passage differences between them, a genome-wide
copy-number and aneuploidy baseline, a homozygous-deletion scan run in both
directions, and an unguided genome-wide search for cut-site-like signatures
together with a swapped-sample control to measure its own background.

3. Reference Construction and Vector Alignment - Yeji Park (2 Hours)
Once the construct sequences were supplied, built the vector references with the
human-homologous regions masked out - the 53BP1 portion of one construct is
human sequence and would otherwise have produced false integration sites - and
ran the additional alignments needed to locate vector-genome junctions.

4. Second Integrative WGS Analysis - Yeji Park (6 Hours)
Full re-analysis against the newly supplied target site and constructs:
base-level confirmation of the intended 202 bp single-allele deletion, its
consequence for the gene, enumeration and read-level inspection of 1,587
off-target candidate sites in both samples, the six paired-cut loci that could
reproduce the intended excision, vector copy number, and vector-genome junction
mapping.
Because the control line carries an integrated Cas9 construct and went through
the same CRISPR treatment, the usual subtraction of control from edited sample
could not be used. Every result therefore had to be produced for both samples
side by side, a reverse-direction control had to be run, and each structural
variant candidate had to be re-examined individually.

5. Client Service and Internal Communication (1.5 Hours)
Communicated with the customer to obtain the sample details, experimental
design, guide sequences and construct maps that were missing from the
submission form, and to establish why the non-ideal control had been used.
Answered technical, statistical and methodological questions arising from the
report. Internal discussion and coordination via WeChat.
